About Me

Hi! I am a Computer Information Systems student concentrating in Cybersecurity at Austin Peay State University (APSU). I also serve as a Teaching and Research Assistant in the CSIT department and serve as President of the APSU Cybersecurity Club.

I am always expanding my knowledge of cybersecurity, general IT, mathematics, and more. This portfolio highlights what I have learned and the experiences along my journey.

Professional Research

During my work with the CSIT department, I collaborated with Dr. Kriti Chauhan on research accepted at the Decision Sciences Institute (DSI) Conference. Our study examined how daily workplace social media usage (categorized into professional, public, and private engagement) impacts employee job and life satisfaction. The findings revealed that using social media for public and professional purposes enhances work-life satisfaction, while personal use during work hours shows no significant impact.

Projects, Leadership, & Experiences

Cybersecurity Club President

As President of the Cybersecurity Club at Austin Peay State University, I lead meetings, organize events, teach cybersecurity concepts, and collaborate with the Computer Science Department along with other computer-related campus organizations. In this process I have established positive relationships with other university's cyber teams, local IT firms, and even national organizations. Most notably, in my time as President, club participation has tripled, and student engagement continues to grow.

Beyond these accomplishments, I find the greatest reward in helping other students develop their skills and discover new opportunities. Serving as President has been an incredible privilege, few experiences have ever been as rewarding as the connections I have gained from this position.

SIEM & Cybersecurity Homelab

I designed and deployed a personal cybersecurity homelab using Proxmox virtualization and Ubuntu Server to simulate real-world IT infrastructure and security operations. Implemented Wazuh SIEM for log collection, system monitoring, and threat detection across the virtual machines. This project allows me to explore both offensive and defensive cybersecurity techniques, investigate suspicious activity, and strengthen my skills in Linux system administration, networking, incident response, and security monitoring. This is an ongoing project that I have continued to expand upon as I develop new skills and learn of more technologies.

PhreakNic 2026

I attended PhreakNIC 26, where I competed in the Capture the Flag (CTF) competition and helped my team secure 7th place overall. I led my team in individual scoring, contributing 3,775 points toward our team's total of 9,175 points. Throughout the competition, I applied and strengthened my cybersecurity skills through hands-on challenges involving problem-solving, technical analysis, security concepts, pattern recognition, cryptography, and interactive real-world challenges. Since this was the first CTF competition for many members of the APSU, I was able to teach them core CTF techinques and mentor them during the competition. Beyond the competition, I had the opportunity to connect with talented individuals in the cybersecurity community who taught me invaluable information. Overall, PhreakNIC 26 was an incredibly rewarding experience that further strengthened my passion for cybersecurity and competitive security challenges.
