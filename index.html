About Me

Driven by a passion for continuous learning and technical growth, I am always actively expanding my knowledge of cybersecurity, general IT, mathematics, and more. This portfolio highlights what I have learned and my experiences.

During my work with the CSIT department, I collaborated with Dr. Kriti Chauhan on research accepted at the Decision Sciences Institute (DSI) Conference. Our study examined how daily workplace social media usage (categorized into professional, public, and private engagement) impacts employee job and life satisfaction. The findings revealed that using social media for public and professional purposes enhances work-life satisfaction, while personal use during work hours shows no significant impact.
